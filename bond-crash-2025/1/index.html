Why This Matters Now

Bonds are the gravity of markets. When long Treasuries move, they pull valuations, credit spreads, and risk appetite with them. Catalysts (JOLTS → ADP → NFP, CPI/PPI, FOMC) can trigger fast re‑pricings. These are the moments when defined‑risk options can turn a clean thesis into asymmetric outcomes — with risk capped at premium.

“When catalysts cluster, timing matters more than conviction. Small, planned positions can do more than big, late ones.”
  • Catalysts bunch up and drive multi‑day swings.
  • Options let you define risk up front.
  • Process first: thesis → levels → sizing → exits → review → scale.

/ZB vs TLT — Reading the Pricing

/ZB — 30‑Year Treasury Bond Futures

  • Quote style: 114’16 = 114 + 16/32. 1 point = $1,000; 1 tick (1/32) = $31.25.
  • Option example: 0’20 = 20/32 = 0.625 points ≈ $625 per contract.

TLT — 20+ Year Treasury ETF

  • Equity‑style options: dollars per share; 1 option = 100 shares.
  • Example: $90 put at $3.00 costs $300; breakeven $87.

Strategies & Risk Controls

Long Put — clean directional exposure; risk capped at premium. Use around catalysts; harvest volatility spikes.

Vertical Put Spread — buy ATM/near‑ATM put; sell lower strike to reduce cost; max gain = strike width − net debit.

  • Start small; scale after process consistency.
  • 1–6 weeks for event plays; 1–6 months for structural moves.
  • Pre‑write exits: targets, invalidations, and time stops.
